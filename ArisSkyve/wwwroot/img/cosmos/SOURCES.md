# Celestial image sources

All imagery in this folder is stored locally for the UI prototype. Keep the
credit information when replacing or redistributing an asset.

## Planets

- `mercury.jpg` — NASA/JHU-APL/Carnegie, MESSENGER. Source: https://science.nasa.gov/solar-system/planets/
- `venus.jpg` — NASA/JPL-Caltech, Mariner 10. Source: https://science.nasa.gov/solar-system/planets/
- `earth.jpg` — NASA, Apollo 17 Blue Marble. Source: https://science.nasa.gov/solar-system/planets/
- `mars.jpg` — NASA/JPL. Source: https://science.nasa.gov/solar-system/planets/
- `jupiter.jpg` — NASA/JPL-Caltech/SwRI/MSSS, Juno. Source: https://science.nasa.gov/solar-system/planets/
- `saturn-hubble.tif` / `saturn-hubble-cutout.png` — NASA, ESA, and STScI, Hubble. `saturn-hubble-cutout.png` is a local crop with the dark canvas removed for UI compositing. Source: https://science.nasa.gov/asset/hubble/saturn/
- `saturn.jpg` / `saturn-cutout.png` — NASA/JPL-Caltech/Space Science Institute, Cassini. Retained as an unused visual candidate. Source: https://science.nasa.gov/solar-system/planets/
- `uranus.jpg` — NASA/JPL-Caltech, Voyager 2. Source: https://science.nasa.gov/solar-system/planets/
- `neptune.jpg` — NASA/JPL-Caltech, Voyager 2. Source: https://science.nasa.gov/image-detail/amf-2a9a3aef-acf0-4334-b13e-9adb839cf331/

## Sun and background

- `sun-304.jpg` — NASA/Goddard Space Flight Center/SDO, AIA 304 Å full disk. Source: https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/10996
- `carina-nebula.png` — NASA/ESA/CSA/STScI, Webb NIRCam + MIRI composite. Source: https://science.nasa.gov/asset/webb/cosmic-cliffs-in-the-carina-nebula-nircam-and-miri-composite-image/

Other downloaded Sun candidates are retained only for visual comparison and
are not loaded by the prototype.

## Earth (home orbital stage)

- `maps/earth-day-8k.jpg`, `maps/earth-day-4k.jpg` — NASA Earth Observatory, Blue
  Marble Next Generation (`world.topo.bathy.200412.3x21600x10800`), downsampled
  locally. Source: https://visibleearth.nasa.gov/images/73909/
- `maps/earth-surface.jpg` — local derivation: R/G hold the tangent-space normal
  built from NASA/GEBCO `gebco_08_rev_elev_21600x10800`, B holds a water mask.
  Source: https://visibleearth.nasa.gov/images/73934/
- `maps/earth-clouds.jpg` — Solar System Scope 8K Earth clouds, CC BY 4.0.
  Source: https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/
- `maps/earth-night.jpg` — Solar System Scope 8K Earth night map, CC BY 4.0.
  Source: https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/
