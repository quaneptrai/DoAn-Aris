## Transparent means visibly see-through

The environment must remain visible through the card. No warm-pearl/cream slab, heavy milky frost, or opaque navy rectangle disguised by a thin transparent border. Use a low-alpha dark glass surface (starting range ~0.08–0.18), restrained blur only if needed, a thin controlled reflective rim and gentle local shading for contrast. Judge actual rendered transparency, not the CSS variable name.

Keep scene detail behind the card sufficiently calm so text remains readable. Use local background attenuation rather than making the entire card opaque. Do not allow large bright bodies to cross through labels or typed text. Particles at the edge should supply the creation gesture, not form permanent visual clutter around every control.

Preserve the redesigned form's deliberate hierarchy, generous spacing, actual brand symbol, visible labels, substantial primary email action and recognizable social choices. Adapt its colors and controls to transparent dark glass. Use high-contrast near-white text, readable helper text, precise translucent inset fields and a cobalt primary action. No decorative badges, fake statistics or invented trust claims.

The accepted-request, loading, invalid-email, retry and simulated social states must live inside the same transparent frame, not switch back to a cream surface. Do not send real email or initiate OAuth.
