## Proposed next iteration: a trajectory with a luminous interior

Keep one large, spatially coherent, asymmetric looping body. Give it a readable opening and a rising tapered end. The body can combine a smooth, softly lit core with particulate edges; do not rely on thousands of equally prominent square points to communicate volume. Use front/back separation, controlled occlusion and localized highlights. Bloom should reveal the form rather than erase its edges. Dust should supply scale, not fill every empty region.

Compose the artwork and typography as one poster-like arrangement. Test a wider headline measure so the desktop headline is not forced into three heavy lines merely because of a narrow text wrapper. Use the sculpture's empty space deliberately, with no collisions across pointer extremes. The form should share the scene's surface and typography language; consider reducing the outer card's visual weight. Keep field boundaries and the primary CTA unmistakable.

Suggested motion sequence, to assess in a recording rather than accept on paper:

- First frame: a legible composition and immediately usable form; no empty loading stage.
- Entrance: a single directional gathering or unveiling over approximately 1.5–2.2 seconds. It should explain the sculpture's shape, not look like particles arriving from arbitrary directions.
- Rest: slow movement within the body and occasional soft highlights, with the overall silhouette stable.
- Pointer: damped, bounded changes of view or local flow inside the artwork region. Pointer movement over the form must not keep steering the sculpture.
- Input focus: ease down ambient energy without snapping or blanking the scene.
- Request accepted: one subtle resolving gesture. This indicates an email request was accepted, not that authentication succeeded. Keep validation errors local to the form.
- Reduced motion: a composed static artwork, not a missing hero. Mobile needs its own crop and form-first arrangement.

Three.js/WebGL remains an appropriate option when it improves volume, materials or rendering efficiency. Canvas is also defensible if the actual result passes visual and performance review. Do not simplify the art merely to avoid WebGL; do not rewrite solely to claim Three.js. Explain a change with observed evidence.
